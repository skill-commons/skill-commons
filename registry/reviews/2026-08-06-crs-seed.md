# CRS review-maturity seed decision

**Policy:** `skill-commons-review-v1`

**Authority:** `skill-commons`

**Decision date:** 2026-08-06

**Accountable reviewer:** Tom Tong (`tomtong2015`)

## Decision

The 22 active skills in `skill-commons/curated-research-skills` are recorded as
`curated` review maturity. This is a structured backfill of the completed Commons
curation program, not a new audit performed on 2026-08-06.

The individual registry records retain their original assessment dates and exact source
commit, path, and directory tree. Repository membership does not assign maturity; each
active record carries this decision explicitly. A future source-tree change requires a
new decision.

## Evidence scope

- **Provenance:** exact source commits and skill-directory trees are pinned in the
  registry; derivation and consolidation history are recorded in the CRS
  `PROVENANCE.md` file.
- **Rights:** authorship, attribution, and licenses received curator review during the
  curation program.
- **Security:** skill scope, secrets, network behavior, commands, and side effects were
  reviewed and, where necessary, rewritten to bounded defaults.
- **Operability:** every retained skill had proportionate repository validation and
  focused tests; heavier scientific paths used bounded integration evidence where
  appropriate.
- **Scientific validity:** supported and excluded uses are documented. This decision does
  not claim independent scientific reproduction for every skill.
- **Reproducibility:** reproducibility instructions or assets are documented, but the
  level varies by workflow and platform. Individual skill boundaries remain
  authoritative.
- **Maintenance:** CRS is the continuing Commons-maintained canonical source for these
  skill versions.

## Limitations

`curated` describes review maturity under this policy. It is not a global trust badge,
runtime authorization, guarantee of security in every environment, or assertion of
universal scientific correctness. Clients must still inspect the pinned skill's declared
scope, capabilities, environment evidence, and excluded uses.
