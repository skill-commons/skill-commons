# ClawHub adapter sketch

ClawHub/OpenClaw entries are external observations, not automatic Commons releases.

The adapter should record native identity, version, source, license signal, attribution,
last-seen time, scan result and any verified mapping to a Commons digest. Raw instructions
are not loaded during remote discovery. Conversion occurs only after local policy permits
the source and the converter can account for every input field and packaged file.

Export may generate a compatible host package for a published Commons release when the
license and capability mapping are lossless. The export retains the Commons coordinate and
digest in round-trip metadata; ClawHub ratings/downloads remain ecosystem observations and
do not become Commons research-validation evidence.
